# Overseas pre-order design preview

Routes: /pre-orders and /pre-orders/design-preview, noindex.

Matches the approved pink/ivory reference with supplied artwork, origin filtering, arrival/price sorting, sample batch journey, product details, bounded quantity selection and computed product total. FAQ and contact links are functional.

All products, dates, prices and shipment updates are clearly sample content. No live carrier integration, reservation, stock deduction, payment or order creation is performed. Continue to pre-order displays this limitation. Warehouse arrival is explicitly distinct from local customer delivery. Commercial deposits and cancellation/refund terms are not invented.

Tests cover filtering/sort immutability, arrival order and quantity/total boundaries. Browser checks cover filters, dialogs, quantities and preview-only continuation. Mobile layout uses two product columns and a vertical shipment timeline in the detail dialog.
