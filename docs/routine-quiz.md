# Routine finder reference implementation

Routes: `/routine-quiz` and `/routine-quiz/design-preview` (noindex).

The first approved reference supplies hero and product artwork through SVG viewBox crops. Layout, headings, selection controls, progress, product cards and dialogs are real HTML. Footer follows the second reference: text-only ANUSMITA, Customer Care / Shop columns and legal links.

Four steps: skin type, up to two concerns, texture/sensitivity preferences, routine. Defaults match the reference (Combination / Pigmentation); users can change every answer. Desktop keeps the reference's concerns and result previews below step one. Mobile presents one step at a time. AM includes cleanser, moisturizer and sunscreen; PM excludes sunscreen. Product details explain which preferences contributed.

Recommendations are illustrative product categories, not stock-backed SKUs, prescriptions or purchasable products. Product CTAs open details and link to the existing contact assistant. No payment, inventory or medical diagnostic system is connected. Live catalogue matching requires curated product metadata and availability validation before launch.

Save is explicit and device-local. Nothing is sent to a server. Stored input is validated before use; restore and removal are explicit, storage failures do not block the quiz. No analytics events include answers.

Keyboard radio controls, pressed-state chips, live selection-limit messages, focus on step changes, accessible native dialogs, reduced-motion support and 320px mobile layouts are included.
