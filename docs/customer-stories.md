# Customer stories design

Routes: `/customer-stories` and `/customer-stories/design-preview` (noindex). Approved artwork supplies the collage, gallery covers and invitation banner through SVG viewBox crops. Text, filters, cards and dialogs are real responsive HTML. The gallery remains explicitly labelled sample imagery; no customer identities, ratings or verified-review claims are invented.

Category and format filters intersect; Recommended and alphabetical sorting are supported. Six reference stories are available. Load more ends with an honest end-of-samples message, not duplicated reviews. Detail dialogs link to the relevant collection. Video cards are sample covers and explicitly report that no video source is connected; there is no simulated playback.

Share your story opens a **local-only** form. JPG/PNG/WebP/MP4/WebM up to 20 MB can be previewed with a caption and optional display name. A rights acknowledgement is required. No upload endpoint, persistence, analytics, publication or moderation queue is connected. Object URLs are revoked when media changes or the dialog closes; closing discards the draft. Actual publication requires separate explicit permission and a backend moderation/upload workflow before launch.

Native dialogs provide keyboard dismissal and focus trapping. Controls have accessible labels, pressed states and live result/validation notices. Mobile layout uses two gallery columns and a single story detail sheet.
